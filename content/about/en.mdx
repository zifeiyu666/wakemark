# About WakeMark

WakeMark wakes up your sleeping bookmarks. It syncs your saved tweets from X/Twitter, tags them by topic with AI, organizes them into lists, and sends you digests on your own schedule.

## Why We Built It

Thousands of people save great tweets every day and never read them again. X/Twitter gives you one unsorted list with no search, no tags, and no reminders. We built WakeMark so your saved content finally gets the second life it deserves.

## What We Do

- **Automatic sync**: Connect once via official X OAuth, and your bookmarks stay up to date on their own.
- **AI auto-tagging**: Every bookmark is read and tagged by topic, so you can filter and search instead of scrolling.
- **Lists**: Organize bookmarks into themed lists and share them with one link.
- **Digests**: Get a clean summary of your recent bookmarks in your inbox, daily or weekly, on your schedule.

## Our Principles

- **Read-only access**: We never post from your account and never see your password.
- **Your data stays yours**: Cancel anytime and keep everything you've synced.
- **Respect your inbox**: Digests are summaries, not link dumps.
